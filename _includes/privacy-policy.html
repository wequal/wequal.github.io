Content you open, create or edit in the app stays in the app's sandbox on your device, or in the location you explicitly choose to save or share it to (for example your photo library, the Files app or another app via the share sheet).

{% if app.self_hosted_server %}

Your self-hosted server

{{ app.name }} is a client for the open-source Home Fileshare server. When you sign in, your username and password are sent only to the server address you entered, over your local network. Files you upload or download travel directly between your device and that server. The server is operated by you or by whoever gave you the address; I have no access to it and no visibility into what you store there.

If you use an HTTP (not HTTPS) server address, traffic on your local network is not encrypted. Use HTTPS if your server supports it.

{% endif %} {% if app.cloud_upload %}

Optional cloud processing

Some features (for example transcription) can send a recording to an online service for processing. This only happens when you explicitly trigger that feature for a specific recording; nothing is uploaded automatically or in the background.

Audio sent for processing is handled by {{ app.cloud_provider }} under their own privacy policy and is used solely to produce the result you requested. I do not retain copies of your recordings or transcripts on any server I control.

If you never use the online features, no recording leaves your device.

{% endif %} {% if app.slug == "callrecorder" %}

Recording other people

Laws about recording conversations vary by country and state; many places require the consent of everyone on the call. You are responsible for complying with the laws that apply to you before recording anyone.

{% endif %} {% if app.analytics %}

Analytics and crash reports

To understand how the app is used and to fix crashes, the app sends anonymous usage events and crash reports to {{ app.analytics_provider }}. This data does not include your recordings, files, contacts or any content you create, and it cannot be used to identify you personally. It consists of information such as the app version, device model, OS version, which screens were used and anonymised crash stack traces.

{% else %}

Analytics

The app does not include any analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Deleting a recording, image or file inside the app, or deleting the app itself, permanently removes that data from your device. Content you have exported to other apps or saved to your photo library is managed by those apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
